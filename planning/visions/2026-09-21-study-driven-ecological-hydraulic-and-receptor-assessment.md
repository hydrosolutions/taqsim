## Potential-track sizing and service conveyance

Apply the following routes in order, retaining applicability, inputs, every failed or missing condition and the next route:

1. **Habitat:** applicable potential objective, surveyed channel, accepted flow–habitat relation, selected threshold/curve point, season and ramping conditions. Evaluate candidates against all specified conditions.
2. **Joint velocity/depth:** an applicable target and defined section, accepted flow/depth/velocity relation over its operating domain, seasonal/ramping limits and downstream connectivity where required. Both depth and velocity must be assessed on the same candidate. Last surveyed flow is not a criterion.
3. **Authenticated service conveyance:** actual timed duties at named offtakes and downstream service points, accepted conveyance/storage relation, boundary conditions, losses and their destinations. An operating rule or approving act supplies the duty first; failing that use the permit entitlement. Observed abstraction is not a duty.
4. **Regulated-body winter share:** suspended. It cannot size an obligation or rescue another route. Do not import the Kazakh winter-share recommendation into this potential profile.

For conveyance, incoming service flow must supply scheduled offtakes/downstream service plus storage change and accepted losses, less other accepted inflows counted once. Keep rates and interval volumes distinct. Seepage to an aquifer/receptor remains a transfer to its named destination. Do not subtract a supposed return before its timing and location are established.

Where losses/storage depend on trial flow, update them through the accepted relation and repeat the balance. Retain initial conditions, every trial, residual, capacity/ramping checks and a predeclared justified convergence tolerance and iteration limit. Accept only a converged, physically supported candidate that can convey all stated duties. Unknown losses are not zero. Missing input, capacity/ramping failure, unsupported reversal and nonconvergence retain failed-route reasons; never return the last trial as an accepted answer. Numerical stopping settings are engineering choices, not invented ecological policy. A service-flow result carries no ecological-adequacy claim.

These routes produce supported seasonal **floor-only** candidates, not a full natural regime or a newly derived delivery obligation. Do not apply the natural annual-minimum floor construction or baseline multi-day gate. Preserve supported active quality contributions and independent existing duties through the handoff to final assembly. Salinity, temperature and sediment requirements remain additional conditions, not alternative fallback sizing routes.

If every applicable route fails, return pending/no computable basis with the tried-route record, missing evidence, responsibility/review information, revisit date and uncertainty status. Do not invent zero or a positive default. A supported zero needs the report's evidence-backed service determination and attributable approval/review conditions. Designed-dry zero remains an explicitly proposed interpretation requiring its adoption/authority conditions and audited service determination; missing evidence never authorizes it. Label hypothetical treatment as such and preserve existing duties. Undefined legal eligibility or service-impact thresholds remain supplied/unresolved inputs, not software defaults.

Unsupported shallow-groundwater dependence retains uncertainty and its missing-model milestone; no artificial-track conservative interim value is borrowed from the natural track. ## Shared hydraulic assessment

Assess named depth, velocity, stage and other supported state variables within their own location and meaning. Stage needs its datum; depth its bed reference; velocity its direction or declared speed magnitude. Local and section-mean velocity differ. Assess current continuity on its specified domain, not a positive-discharge flag.

A relation retains geometry/version, boundary conditions, valid range, permitted interpolation, uncertainty and intended-use acceptance. Do not silently extrapolate or ignore relevant backwater, hysteresis or changing geometry. Evaluate every required condition on the same candidate. Missing qualitative criteria remain unassessed unless an attributable supported specialist finding supplies the test.

For a declared discrete transition, `r = (x_current - x_previous) / dt` with actual strictly positive elapsed time. Compare against separate nonnegative rise/fall magnitudes, preserving strict/inclusive endpoints. An intentionally absent bound differs from a missing required one. Absolute steps, relative percentages and instantaneous derivatives are different operators and require their own definition or supported imported result.

Endpoint rates concern only the declared transition statistic, not unseen peak rates. Differences between interval means concern those means. Daily means cannot certify within-day conditions. Declare timestamp convention, expected coverage and predecessor history. Irregular supported adjacent samples may supply a discrete rate; missing expected samples cannot be bridged. Missing predecessors remain unassessed; never wrap a record. A season-boundary transition needs an applicable criterion or supported subdivision, not invented intermediate states.

For supported state intervals `[l_previous,u_previous]` and `[l_current,u_current]`, conservative rate bounds are `[(l_current-u_previous)/dt, (u_current-l_previous)/dt]`. Supported joint error evidence may tighten them; no independence or probability is inferred. Full containment within allowed bounds passes; disjointness fails; overlap is indeterminate, with endpoint strictness preserved. Apply the same interval logic to depth/velocity ranges.

Return criterion, value/interval, location, time pair, change magnitude, elapsed time, domain, support, coverage, finding and reason. Preserve directional/component findings losslessly. A supported required failure survives unknown components as failure with incomplete coverage. With no supported failure, unresolved required checks prevent complete pass. Passing the declared tests is not ecological certification or complete sanitary compliance.

### State-to-inflow conversion

Use accepted level–area–storage or other supported physical relations to connect initial conditions and inflow schedules to the target trajectory. For a storage target, managed arrival volume is target storage change plus outflows/losses minus other accepted inflows over the same interval. Rainfall and groundwater enter once with their direction and timing. A negative residual is a surplus with possible outflow/operating implications, not negative delivery or proof of target satisfaction.

Map arrival needs to the real control point and schedule with supported travel time, losses, capacity, ramping, initial/final storage and delayed exchanges. If head, area or hydroperiod lacks an accepted storage representation, assess supplied candidate schedules through the appropriate supported relation/model. A river discharge alone is insufficient.

Several unknown inflows cannot be uniquely allocated from one balance. Preserve feasible schedules/assumptions or unresolved allocation until a responsible operating choice is supplied; do not optimise or invent shares. Reconcile pathway estimates against the same receptor state rather than summing independently sized totals that already assume other inflows. A pathway's current contribution share cannot scale down its requirement. Substitution needs evidence of the same state, period, quality and ecological process.

Capacity or target failure means infeasible, not a smaller requirement. Missing targets, relations or operating selection leave attributable components unsized, not zero or compliant.

### Water, salt and evidence

Preserve initial inventories, all inflows/outflows, evaporation, losses, delays and exchanges. For interval amounts, `V_next = V + I - O - E` and `M_next = M + mass_in - mass_out + separate_load + represented_reaction_or_exchange`. Avoid double-counting loads already in carriers. Under the nonvolatile conservative-salt assumption, evaporation carries no salt. Unsupported processes need supported imports or explicit limitations; a conservative-ion screen does not supply reactive/thermal/stratified physics.

For a supported representative mixed compartment with positive volume, `C = M/V`. A bulk average does not establish root-zone or spatial concentration. At zero volume aqueous concentration is undefined; retain mass and do not assume remobilisation on refilling. Conservation residuals and precision remain visible. A failed physical balance invalidates affected support; arithmetic closure alone does not establish model suitability.

Evaluate quantity and salinity jointly for the same candidate/scenario, receptor and declared period. Endpoint success does not prove intervening exposure or interval maxima. A supported required failure remains visible with incomplete coverage. No known failure plus unresolved required evidence means indeterminate; all required tests passing within their supported domains gives only configured-target satisfaction. Empty required target sets cannot yield vacuous pass. Unsupported numerical failure remains exploratory, not a supported verdict.

### Coincidence and duration

For non-overlapping intervals with whole-interval support, cumulative suitable duration is `sum(dt * indicator(quantity AND salinity))`, not the two separate totals. Point samples do not certify whole intervals.

With unknown intervals, the lower bound counts known joint passes; the upper bound also includes intervals not ruled out by a known required failure. For a minimum-duration criterion, a lower bound meeting the criterion passes, an upper bound below it fails, and overlap is indeterminate; preserve strictness. Such bounds may resolve that duration test despite incomplete raw coverage, without filling gaps or certifying other tests.

For consecutive duration, compute the longest adjoining suitable run: unknowns excluded for the lower bound and potentially suitable for the upper bound. Do not substitute cumulative duration, bridge known gaps or wrap the assessment boundary. Missing required boundary history remains a limitation. Other exposure definitions require a declared supported operator.

A quantity-satisfied, salinity-failed receptor fails its specified joint targets. This does not automatically size corrective releases, activate dormant quality, alter an issued duty or establish ecological/legal status. More water need not improve every target. Keep quality activation/application evidence and explicitly hypothetical activation distinct.
